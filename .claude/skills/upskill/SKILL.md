# upskill

## Precedent
Gap analysis between your profile and your target roles, with a prioritised learning plan.
A career-planning tool, not just job search.

## Inputs
`profile/*`, tracked postings (or a single JD passed in).

## Output
- Prioritized **heatmap** of skill gaps (frequency across target JDs × your current depth).
- A learning plan: web-searched resources + realistic time estimates per gap.
- Honest framing at senior level: claiming altitude you can't evidence backfires — the plan
  builds real evidence, it doesn't coach you to fake it.
