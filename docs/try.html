What happens behind the scenes

It's not just rewriting your CV.

This page is 1 stage of a larger funnel, and even here it's doing more than swapping in keywords. Before it hands you anything, it works through a set of rules and conventions, the same ones the full system uses.

  • Grounds every line in what you gave it. It selects, reorders, and rephrases the facts already in your CV. It never invents an employer, a title, a date, or a metric to fit the posting.
  • Reads the posting for what it's really asking. It maps the role's stated needs to the evidence you actually have, and leans on that, rather than padding with things you can't back up.
  • Writes in a plain human voice. No em-dashes, no "results-driven / passionate" filler, none of the usual AI tells. It's meant to read like you wrote it.
  • Flags anything it can't trace. The honesty badge you see is the browser version of the code-enforced gate: any number or claim it can't tie back to your CV gets surfaced for you to check, not quietly shipped.

And this is only the "tailored documents" stage. The full system runs the whole funnel:

Discover roles Judge fit Tailor documents Find a warm path in Apply Track Interview prep Offer & negotiate

The warm-intro step is the one almost nothing else touches. It finds a real path into a company through your own network, before you apply.

Why this exists

Job hunting is a funnel. Most tools only fix 1 stage.

You discover roles, judge fit, tailor documents, find a way in, apply, track, prep, and negotiate. Most tools automate one of those and leave you to stitch the rest together by hand. Lucy is a single conversational agent that manages the whole funnel, refuses to fabricate anything about you, and always stops before committing you to anything.
