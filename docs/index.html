Why this exists

Applying to a job is the easy half. Everyone can see the posting; everyone sends the same tailored CV.

The half that actually moves the needle is quieter: knowing someone on the inside. A warm introduction changes your odds more than any keyword ever will — and it's the part no tool would touch.

So the job search isn't one problem. It's a funnel of six — and most tools solve exactly one.

Discovery. Fit. Tailoring. The warm path in. The interview. The offer. Each stage needs different work, and stitching them together by hand is where good candidates lose momentum. Lucy holds the whole funnel in one place, so you spend your energy on judgment, not logistics.

Why me

I didn't build this because AI agents are having a moment. I built it because I've spent years on the other side of this problem — coaching people through their job search and watching where they get stuck.

Before Lucy, I was a coach at Pragmatic Builders, ran resume and interview sessions, and mentored people through career moves — friends, colleagues, strangers who found me. The same patterns came up every time: strong candidates freezing on what to say, great fits never heard back from, and the quiet truth that a warm introduction beats a perfect application. Lucy is that hard-won knowledge, turned into a system.

Three rules it won't break

What makes it trustworthy.

/ 01

Never fabricates

Every generated line is checked in code against a frozen record of your real history. Invent an employer or a metric and the build fails. A promise you can verify.

/ 02

You commit, not the agent

It automates everything up to applying, reaching out, or accepting — then stops and hands you the wheel. No message leaves without your say-so.

/ 03

Your data stays yours

Your real profile never leaves your machine. The public repo ships only templates, so anyone can fork it and fill in their own.
